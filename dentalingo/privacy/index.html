Privacy Policy

For Dentalingo: Dental Assistant (iOS).
Effective 22 August 2026 · Last updated 22 August 2026

The short version: Dentalingo does not collect, transmit, store or share any personal data. There is no user account, no analytics, no advertising SDK and no tracking of any kind. The app contains no networking code — everything it does happens on your device.

1. Who this policy is from

Dentalingo is an independent iOS application published by the developer of this site. Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@laborsam.com.

2. Information the app collects

None. Specifically, Dentalingo does not collect:

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers or any contact information;
  • account credentials — the app has no sign-up, sign-in or user account at all;
  • device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA) or fingerprinting signals;
  • location data;
  • usage analytics, crash telemetry, session recordings or heatmaps;
  • contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data — the app never requests access to any of these.

Dentalingo integrates no third-party analytics, attribution, advertising or social-media SDKs. It makes no network requests of its own.

3. Information the app stores on your device

To work as a study app, Dentalingo saves your learning state in a private database inside the app's own storage container on your iPhone:

  • which lessons and atlas modules you have completed;
  • your answers, correct/incorrect history and spaced-repetition review schedule;
  • XP, streak, daily goal and quest progress;
  • mock exam attempts and scores;
  • app settings such as sound and notification preferences;
  • which components you have unlocked.

This data stays on your device. It is not uploaded to us or to anyone else — we operate no server that could receive it, and we never see it.

4. Your own iCloud or computer backups

If you have enabled iOS device backup, the app's on-device data may be included in your encrypted backup to iCloud or your computer, the same as for any other app. That backup belongs to you and is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we have no access to it. You can exclude Dentalingo from backups in iOS Settings.

5. Purchases

Dentalingo offers one-time in-app purchases. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or have access to your payment card details, billing address or Apple Account information. The app checks your entitlements locally using Apple's StoreKit framework to decide what to unlock.

Apple provides developers with aggregated, anonymised sales and download reports. These reports do not identify individual customers. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Notifications

Dentalingo can send optional study reminders. These are local notifications scheduled on your device by iOS — there is no push server and no device token is registered or transmitted. The app asks for notification permission only after you have finished your first lesson, never at launch, and it works fully without it. You can turn reminders off at any time in iOS Settings or in the app's Profile tab.

7. Links to other sites

The app's credits screen and this website link to outside pages — image sources on Wikimedia Commons, open-source project repositories, licence deeds and reference documents. Once you follow such a link you are on a site we do not control and this policy no longer applies.

8. Children

Dentalingo is a professional certification study tool intended for adults preparing for dental assisting exams. It is not directed to children, and it is not designed for or marketed to children under 13. Because the app collects no data whatsoever, it collects no data from children either.

9. Your rights, and how to delete your data

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data a company holds about you. We hold none — there is nothing for us to look up, hand over, correct or erase, and nothing to sell or share. We do not sell or share personal information under any definition.

To erase the data on your device, delete the app from your iPhone. iOS removes the app's storage container and all learning progress with it. This is permanent and cannot be undone by us. Your past purchases remain tied to your Apple Account and can be restored with "Restore Purchases" if you reinstall.

10. Security

Because nothing is transmitted, there is no transmission to intercept and no server of ours to breach. On-device data is protected by iOS app sandboxing and by your device passcode and encryption.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app ever gains a feature that changes what data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the "Last updated" date at the top will change. Material changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.

12. Contact

Privacy questions, requests or complaints: hello@laborsam.com. We aim to reply within 3 business days.
